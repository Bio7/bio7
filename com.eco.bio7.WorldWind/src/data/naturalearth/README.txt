Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain (places version 5.1.2, boundaries version 5.1.0):
- ne_10m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land: the land boundaries of the countries (only the attribute FEATURECLA).
- ne_10m_populated_places.csv: the populated places (ne_10m_populated_places_simple: name, latitude,
  longitude, scalerank, adm0cap, pop_max).
Used by the layers "Political Boundaries" and "Place Names" of the globe (made with Natural Earth).
